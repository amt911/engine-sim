# Research addendum (2026-10-21): beating engine-sim on realism

Searched for work newer than the main research doc. What changes the plan:

| Finding | Source | Use here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large exhaust pulses **steepen into shocks**; nonlinearity moves energy into higher harmonics as amplitude grows — exactly what linear waveguides can't do (and why WOT should sound harsher/louder than coast) | MOC two-stroke exhaust noise study, [TRID 181750](https://trid.trb.org/View/181750); finite-difference quasi-1D duct, [NTRS 19840046908](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19840046908); finite-amplitude standing waves, [NPS Calhoun](https://calhoun.nps.edu/entities/publication/d6926f22-fe1f-4bee-b351-c3d8a1c4a9de) | Priority for the 1D finite-volume exhaust (HLLC/MUSCL) — or, cheaper, a nonlinear waveguide where c depends on local amplitude (c + (γ+1)/2·u) |
| Wall losses in ducts come from the **Stokes boundary layer** (∝ √ω), not bulk viscosity | [APS DFD 2019 Q06.3](https://meetings-archive.aps.org/dfd/2019/q06/3) | Frequency-dependent pipe loss (√f) instead of a flat loss per metre |
| **Pulse-train + resonators**, combustion noise switched by torque polarity (on/overrun), physics-shaped envelopes; listening test: naive listeners rate it as real, experts can tell | Doerfler & Wyse, [arXiv 2603.09391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09391) | Overrun vs load regimes; listening-test protocol for our own validation |
| Harmonic + stochastic components with **rpm/torque gain curves**, learned from data, deployable as plain DSP | EONE, [arXiv 2606.21521](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21521) | Calibration layer: fit noise/harmonic gains of our voice to real recordings (LFA) |
| 19 h engine dataset with rpm/torque labels | [arXiv 2603.07584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07584) | Reference recordings for calibration (licence: NC) |
| Ange's new sim: movable microphone around the engine, live pipe tuning | [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/new-3d-engine-sim-can-synthesize-super-realistic-exhaust-sounds-in-real-time) | Multiple listener positions (step 4) |
